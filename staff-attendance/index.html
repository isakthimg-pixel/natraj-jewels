Who can mark attendance

Each person gets their own PIN. Without a PIN, anyone can look at attendance but cannot change it. The app signs out by itself after 5 minutes without use. Every change records who made it.

The PIN lock stops people without a PIN from changing attendance on this device. Records are stored only on this device, so keep it in the shop or with you.

Shop settings

Backup

Attendance is saved in this browser on this device only. Download a backup once a week and keep it safe, for example in Google Drive or on WhatsApp to yourself. Clearing browser data erases records that are not backed up.

Moving to a new phone or computer? Download a backup here, open this page on the new device, and restore it.

Birthday and anniversary list

One sheet for each month, with the columns Birthday, Anniversary, Ref no, Name and Contact no. Dates are read as day/month/year, and the sheet’s month is trusted when a date looks mixed up. People with the same phone and name are joined into one; a family on one phone become separate people. People already on the list only get the dates they are missing, and customers already entered on the form are left out. Nothing is saved until you press the button.

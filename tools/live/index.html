Each staff member is a chess piece, from their designation in People & settings: gate keeper a pawn, accountant and manager a rook, sales a bishop, all-rounders a knight. Drag to turn, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap a piece to follow it. Signed in on one of the three store computers (chosen in People & settings, under devices): it stands at that computer and its gem glows gold. On a phone or any other device: a small screen floats in front of it. Not marked yet: it waits at the gate. At thottam: it tends the crops on the farm island. At home: it sits outside the house on the home island. Absent, on leave or week off: it rests on the bench on the small island.

Owner only

Shop settings

Weekly holiday

Shaded in the attendance register and skipped when leave is approved.

Daily rate update

The people ticked under “Daily rate” in Who can sign in enter the gold & silver rate every day. Until one of them does, it shows as due on their home page and on your dashboard.

Shop hours

The Live shop shows the shop open or closed; after closing, staff who are not signed in have gone home.

Two-step sign-in

Protects your owner account even if someone guesses your PIN: after the PIN, you also type a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or similar; all free). The code changes every 30 seconds.

Recovery code

If you forget your owner PIN or lose the phone with your authenticator app, the recovery code lets you choose a new PIN (and switches two-step sign-in off until you set it up again). Make a new code if the old one is lost or someone else has seen it.

Backup

Records are saved online in your Supabase database. Download a copy of everything once a week and keep it safe.

Devices staff can use

Approve the shop’s computer here, on that computer. Then switch on the lock, and staff can use the apps only on approved devices; on their own phones or at home they see nothing. Owners can use any device. The “Apply for leave” page stays open on any phone.

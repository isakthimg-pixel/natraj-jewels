NATRAJ JEWELS STAFF ATTENDANCE - HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE
=====================================================

This folder is a complete app on its own. It is separate from the
natrajjewels.com website and does not need it.

What's in this folder
  index.html             the app
  img/                   logo and the red texture
  favicon.ico, favicon-32.png, apple-touch-icon.png   icons

1. Put it online with Netlify (free, about 5 minutes)
   - Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up (Google sign-in works).
   - Drag this whole staff-attendance folder onto the page.
   - Netlify gives the app an address like
     https://something-random.netlify.app
   - Under "Site configuration" > "Change site name", choose a name,
     e.g. natraj-attendance -> https://natraj-attendance.netlify.app
   To update the app later: open the site in Netlify, go to "Deploys"
   and drag the updated folder onto it. Saved attendance is not
   affected, because it lives on the shop device, not on Netlify.

2. First time on the shop phone or tablet
   - Open the address, set the owner PIN and WRITE DOWN the recovery code.
   - Settings > Add a person: give PINs to the people who mark attendance.
   - Phone: Share > Add to Home Screen, so it opens like an app.

3. Always open the same address
   Records are saved for one exact web address on one device. Always
   open the app from the home-screen icon or one bookmark. Download a
   backup from Settings once a week and keep it safe.

Using the app
   - Daily: tap Present, Home, Thottam or Half day for each person.
     The Leave button opens a list: Absent, Leave applied or Week off.
     "Days worked" = Present + Home + Thottam, plus half for a Half day.
   - Leave: staff apply for leave themselves (no PIN needed). Someone
     with a PIN approves it and those days are marked "Leave applied"
     (or Half day). Weekly holidays are skipped.
   - Monthly register: month grid with totals per person. "Download
     Excel (CSV)" opens in Excel or Google Sheets for salary.
   - Staff (owner): add staff; when someone leaves press Deactivate
     (keeps their history) rather than Delete.
   - PIN lock: without a PIN the app is view only. It signs out after
     5 minutes without use. Every change records who made it.

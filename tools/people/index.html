Daily rate update

The people ticked under “Daily rate” in Who can sign in enter the gold & silver rate every day. Until one of them does, it shows as due on their home page and on your dashboard.

If you forget your owner PIN, the recovery code lets you choose a new one. Make a new code if the old one is lost or someone else has seen it.

Backup

Records are saved online in your Supabase database. Download a copy of everything once a week and keep it safe.

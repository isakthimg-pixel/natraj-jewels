Our story

Generations of trust on Eswaran Kovil Street

For 90 years Natraj Jewels has been part of Tiruppur's weddings, festivals and family milestones. Many families who buy from us today were brought here by their grandparents.

We started with a simple belief: jewellery should be of honest quality, beautifully made, and worth keeping for a lifetime. We still work that way, with designs for both classic tastes and today's lifestyles.

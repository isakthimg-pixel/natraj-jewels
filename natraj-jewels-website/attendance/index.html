Backup

Attendance is saved in this browser on this device only. Download a backup regularly (once a week is a good habit) and keep it safe, for example in Google Drive or WhatsApp to yourself. Clearing browser data will erase records that are not backed up.

To move to a new phone or computer: download a backup here, open this page on the new device, and restore it.
